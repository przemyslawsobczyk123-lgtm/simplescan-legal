Your privacy in SimpleScan PDF — frequently asked questions

Last updated: 2026-08-08

This page answers the most common questions about data and privacy in SimpleScan PDF. Full legal information can be found in the Privacy Policy.

Are my documents sent to a server?

No. All PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, TXT files and images that you view or process in the Application remain solely on your device. The Application does not copy, send, or share their content with any servers — neither ours nor third-party ones.

Does the Application require an account or login?

No. SimpleScan PDF works without an account. There is no login, no registration, no cloud sync.

Does camera scanning send photos to Google?

No. Scanning uses the Google ML Kit Document Scanner library, but all processing (edge detection, perspective correction, cropping) takes place entirely on your device — offline. Scan images do not leave the phone.

What exactly do you collect?

The Application uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, which collect:

  • basic usage events (opening the app, opening a document — only the file type, e.g. "pdf", without name or content),
  • the number of documents found when scanning folders (only the count, not the names),
  • technical device data (model, Android version, Application version),
  • crash reports (stack trace when the Application crashes).

We do not collect: file names, paths, document contents, contacts, GPS location, browser history.

Does the Application show ads?

For the first 90 days after installation — no. During that period no ad requests are sent.

After 90 days, you may see:

  • a banner at the bottom of the documents list screen,
  • a full-screen ad after closing the fifth document in a row.

Ads never appear while reading a document.

How can I turn off personalised ads?

If you are in the European Economic Area, the first time an ad is shown the Application will ask you for consent via the Google UMP form. You can refuse — ads will then be shown without personalisation.

To change the decision later:

Settings → Ad privacy options

(the option is only shown to users in regions where consent is required).

How can I reset my Advertising ID?

Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset advertising ID

You can also disable ad personalisation completely in the same place.

How can I remove all my data from the Application?

Three ways:

  1. Clear local history: Application settings → Clear history.
  2. Clear Application data: Android Settings → Apps → SimpleScan PDF → Storage → Clear data.
  3. Uninstall the Application: this removes all local metadata and settings.

Will my documents be removed if I clear the data?

Your original files on the device (e.g. in the Download folder) will NOT be removed. The Application only stores metadata (names, paths, settings) in its database — that is what will be cleared. The files remain.

The exception is documents created directly in the Application (scans, edited PDFs) — those go into the system folder Documents/SimpleScan PDF/ and remain there until you delete them yourself.

Are my files encrypted?

The local metadata database is located in the Application's private directory, inaccessible to other apps on your device (Android sandbox isolation).

The Application itself does not encrypt PDF file contents, but offers the "Protect PDF with password" tool (Tools → Protect PDF) which lets you apply an AES password to a chosen file.

Is the Application safe for children?

The Application is not directed at children under 13 years of age and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

Why does the Application ask for access to all files?

So that it can automatically find and show all your PDF/Word/Excel/TXT documents located in various folders on the device. Without this permission you will only see documents that you open manually.

The Application only reads the metadata of those files to display them in the list — it does not copy, modify, or send their content.

You can refuse this permission or revoke it in Android Settings — the Application will still work, just with a limited view of files.
